# Susto del cartel del Circo Maldito

Al abrir el cartel de Giggles, el payaso aparece tras 2,8 segundos de lectura durante 1,15 segundos. Se reproduce un grito procedural a través del control de audio existente. Escape descarta el susto y deja el cartel abierto. Cerrar el cartel antes de tiempo o cambiar de pestaña cancela el temporizador.

El susto es local, una vez por jugador y ronda; sessionStorage conserva ese estado tras recargar. Reiniciar la misión lo habilita de nuevo. La animación respeta `prefers-reduced-motion`.

Recurso: `public/images/clown-screamer.png`. Generado con la herramienta integrada de la skill `imagegen`.

Prompt final:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: fullscreen jump-scare image for a horror circus browser escape-room game. Create a single terrifying original circus clown face lunging directly toward camera, mouth open in a menacing scream, chalk-white cracked theatrical greasepaint, deep black eye sockets with small piercing yellowish pupils, red nose, smeared red mouth makeup and dark crimson hair at both sides. Cinematic realistic horror game concept art, extreme close-up, centered symmetrical face dominating a square 1024x1024 composition, forehead, eyes and open mouth clearly readable. Background near-black darkness, strong dramatic side light and subtle red edge light. Very scary but no gore, no blood, no exposed wounds, no weapons, no text, no lettering, no watermark. Original clown design, no recognizable movie character. Face should remain legible at a brief glance on mobile as well as desktop.
